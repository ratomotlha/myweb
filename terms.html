Liquor Act

Right of Admission is strictly Reserved. In accordance with the South African Liquor Act 59 of 2003, alcohol is never sold or served to persons under the age of 18. Valid original identification (SA ID card/book, driver’s licence, or passport) may be requested at the door or bar, and late-night entertainment sessions may carry strict 18+ or 21+ entry policies as advertised. Neat smart-casual or nightlife attire applies on event evenings; outside food, beverages, and prohibited items are not permitted on the premises.
